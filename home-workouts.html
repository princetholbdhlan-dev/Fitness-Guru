10 Effective Home Workouts Without Equipment

Published on Sept 2026 | By Fitness Guru Team

You don't need an expensive gym membership or heavy weight machines to build strength and burn fat. Bodyweight training is one of the most natural and effective ways to get fit right from the comfort of your home.

1. Push-Ups

Push-ups are a classic full-body exercise that targets your chest, shoulders, triceps, and core. Aim for 3 sets of 10 to 15 reps. Beginners can start with knee push-ups.

2. Bodyweight Squats

Squats build leg strength in your quadriceps, hamstrings, and glutes. Keep your feet shoulder-width apart, chest up, and lower yourself as if sitting on an invisible chair.

3. Planks for Core Strength

Planks build stability across your entire core. Hold a forearm plank for 30 to 60 seconds while keeping your back straight and belly tight.

4. Jumping Jacks

A great full-body cardio movement to elevate your heart rate, boost endurance, and ignite fat burning.

5. Lunges

Lunges improve balance and build unilateral leg strength. Step forward with one leg and lower your hips until both knees are bent at a 90-degree angle.

Pro Tip: Perform this circuit 3 to 4 times a week, staying consistent with rest days and a balanced diet for best results.
