High Protein Diet Plan for Muscle Building

Published on Sept 2026 | By Fitness Guru Team

Protein is the primary building block for muscles and body recovery. Whether your goal is to gain lean mass or lose fat, optimizing your protein intake is essential.

Best Natural Protein Sources

Combine a mix of animal and plant-based protein sources like eggs, chicken breast, fish, paneer, Greek yogurt, lentils, beans, and sprouts.

Timing Your Meals

Spread your protein intake evenly across 3 to 4 meals throughout the day to maximize muscle protein synthesis.

Don't Forget Healthy Fats & Carbs

A balanced diet needs complex carbohydrates (oats, brown rice, whole wheat) for sustained workout energy, along with healthy fats (nuts, seeds) for hormone production.
